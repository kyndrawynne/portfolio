About Kyndra

I've always been drawn to systems—how things work, where they break down, and how to make them better. That curiosity led me to study computer science, where I quickly realized that technology isn't just about writing code—it's about how people interact with it.

I started my career in technical support at Microsoft, working directly with high-value business customers to troubleshoot complex issues. That experience shaped how I think about design—because when you spend every day helping people navigate confusing systems, you start to see the gaps, the friction points, the places where technology fails its users. I didn't just want to fix broken experiences—I wanted to design them better from the start.

That’s what led me to front-end development and product design. I love creating clean, intuitive, and accessible user experiences—whether that means designing an interface that feels effortless or writing the code that makes it work seamlessly. I’ve worked on everything from event websites and e-commerce platforms to interactive dashboards and mobile apps, always focusing on how design and functionality come together to improve the user’s experience.

I recently graduated with a Bachelors in Computer Science, and I’m focused on growing into a role where I can bridge the gap between design and development. My goal is simple: to build products that don’t just look good but feel good to use.
